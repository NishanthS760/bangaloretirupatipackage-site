Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about our platform model

What exactly is BangaloreTirupatiPackage.com? Are you a bus operator?
NO, we are NOT a bus operator. We are an independent booking PLATFORM and AGGREGATOR SERVICE (like RedBus or MakeMyTrip). We partner with 6+ licensed travel operators who own and operate the buses. Our platform verifies operator legitimacy, facilitates booking coordination, provides exclusive Expert TTD Guide Service, negotiates group discounts, and offers 24/7 support.
Who provides the bus and transportation services?
Physical transportation services are provided by our verified partner operators — independent licensed travel companies with valid Karnataka RTO permits (Balaji Tours, Sri Travels, Venkateswara Travels, Tirumala Tours, Express Travels, Premium Tours). We verify all operators for licenses, insurance, and ratings (minimum 4+ stars).
What makes your platform different from booking directly with a travel agent?
(1) Choice — Compare 6+ operators, (2) Verification — We vet operators for licenses & insurance, (3) Exclusive Guide Service — Expert TTD Guide (50% faster darshan), (4) Group Discounts — Up to 13% savings, (5) Scam Protection — Operator verification, (6) 24/7 Support — Centralized platform coordination.
How do platform group discounts work?
2–3 seats: 5% instant discount. 4+ seats: 5% base + extra 2% per person. Example: 6 seats = 5% + (2% × 4 extra) = 13% off. 7+ seats: Contact platform (+91-8088121938) for custom group rates.
What is Expert TTD Guide Service? Is this provided by the operator?
NO, this is provided EXCLUSIVELY by our platform (not by transport operators). Our expert guides have 10+ years temple experience and provide SSD token queue expertise reducing darshan time from 4–6 hours to 2–3 hours (50% faster). Operators do NOT provide this service.
What's included in the ₹2,499 package?
Complete package: AC sleeper bus (verified partner operator), private fresh-up rooms, breakfast, lunch, Expert TTD Guide Service (platform exclusive), regular darshan assistance, prasadam, bottled water, doorstep Bangalore drop, 24/7 platform support coordination. Zero hidden charges.
Are you affiliated with TTD (Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams)?
NO. We are an independent private booking platform, NOT affiliated with or authorized by TTD. We coordinate bookings with private licensed travel operators. Our guides assist with regular darshan queues using expertise, but we do NOT provide any special TTD entry or government-sponsored services.
What is your cancellation policy?
48+ hours before trip: 100% refund. 24–48 hours: 50% refund. Within 24 hours: No refund (operator policies apply). Medical emergencies: Full refund with valid certificate. Contact platform support at +91-8088121938.
