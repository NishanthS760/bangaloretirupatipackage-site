What documents are required for darshan?

Government ID mandatory: Aadhar Card, Passport, PAN Card, or Driver License. TTD requires proof of identity for all pilgrims. Non-Indian nationals need valid passport and visa. Children below 5 years may not need ID but recommended to carry birth certificate.

What is the dress code for temple?

Men: Dhoti/Pyjama with Kurta or traditional wear (No shorts, T-shirts, jeans, western wear). Women: Saree/Churidar/Salwar with Dupatta (No jeans, sleeveless, tight clothing). Our Expert TTD Guide will brief you on proper attire and temple protocols before darshan.
