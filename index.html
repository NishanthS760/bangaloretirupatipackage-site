Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking

What's included in the one-day Tirupati package?

Includes AC sleeper bus with individual berths, private fresh-up rooms (NOT shared), breakfast, lunch, regular darshan assistance, experienced local guide, prasadam, and doorstep drop in Bangalore.

Is TTD Special Entry Darshan (₹300) included?

NO. Official TTD Special Entry Darshan is NOT available. We provide verified Regular Darshan queue assistance only. No premium passes or special entries.

What about AC Sleeper Bus features?

Individual Berths: Upper and lower berths. Charging: USB & 12V charging at each berth. Luggage: Safe storage below. Washroom: Onboard toilet. Comfort: Climate-controlled cabin.

What is the dress code for Tirupati darshan?

Men: Dhoti/Pyjama with Kurta (No shorts, T-shirts, or western wear). Women: Saree/Churidar with Dupatta (No jeans or sleeveless). Children must follow temple dress code.

What documents are required?

Government ID (Mandatory): Aadhar, Passport, PAN, or Driver License. TTD requires proof of identity for darshan. Non-Indian nationals need valid passport & visa.

What is your cancellation policy?

48+ hours before trip: 100% refund. 24–48 hours: 50% refund. Within 24 hours: No refund. Medical emergencies: Full refund with proof.

What meals are provided?

Breakfast: South Indian (Idli/Dosa/Upma). Lunch: Andhra-style vegetarian thali. Special Dietary: Pure vegetarian, Brahmin/Jain certified, no onion/garlic options available on request.
