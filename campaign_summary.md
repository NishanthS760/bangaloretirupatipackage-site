## Generated Output Files (CSV for Bulk Upload)

The following CSV files have been generated in the `data/` directory, ready for bulk upload to Google Ads:

- **`google_ads_campaign_campaigns.csv`**:
  - Contains the main campaign settings, including budget, bidding strategy (Maximize Conversions with a Target CPA), geo-targeting (Bangalore), and campaign type (Search Network Only).
  - Also includes audience targeting suggestions, device bid adjustments, and ad scheduling recommendations.

- **`google_ads_campaign_ad_groups.csv`**:
  - Lists all ad groups created within the campaign, categorized by themes like "One Day Package," "Bus Package," "Car Package," "Tirupati Darshan," and "General."
  - Includes specific SKAGs for high-intent keywords to ensure maximum relevance.

- **`google_ads_campaign_keywords.csv`**:
  - Details all keywords generated for each ad group, along with their match types (Exact, Phrase, Broad) and suggested maximum CPC bids.
  - Keywords are tailored to Bangalore to Tirupati travel packages, covering various search intents.

- **`google_ads_campaign_ads.csv`**:
  - Contains the responsive search ad copy for each ad group, featuring multiple headlines and descriptions dynamically generated from the website's KSPs and pricing.
  - Ads include strong calls-to-action and urgency triggers.

- **`google_ads_campaign_negative_keywords.csv`**:
  - Provides a list of common negative keywords to prevent ads from showing for irrelevant searches, thereby improving ad spend efficiency.

- **`google_ads_campaign_ad_extensions.csv`**:
  - Outlines the configuration for various ad extensions, including Sitelinks (e.g., Book Now, Group Discounts), Callouts (e.g., 50% Faster Darshan, Verified Operators), Structured Snippets (Service offerings), and Call Extensions.
